## Start here

Roleplay Manager runs on your computer. Your characters, chats, memories,
summaries and images live in a SQLite database inside `data/`, and the app talks
to the AI provider you choose.

If this is your first time:

1. [Install the portable package](/docs/getting-started/installation/windows/).
2. [Connect Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible provider](/docs/guides/providers).
3. [Create your first character and chat](/docs/getting-started/first-steps).
